Privacy Policy

Skillowkey is a static index. We do not run analytics cookies, do not sell data, and do not store personal information about visitors.

Search is client-side

All searching and filtering happens entirely in your browser. Your search terms, filters, and page interactions never leave your device or get sent to any server.

What we may observe

Our hosting provider and the Cloudflare network may record standard, transient technical logs (IP address, user agent, requested resource) for security and performance. These are not used to identify you and are not shared with third parties for advertising.

External links

The skills we index link to third-party repositories (primarily GitHub). Once you leave Skillowkey, those sites govern their own data collection. This policy does not apply to them.
